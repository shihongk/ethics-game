# Reflection questions — Scrap-E's Dilemma

As of v0.5, the in-app reflection screen no longer asks these directly —
it hands the student off to the slides/external platform instead (see
`CLAUDE.md`). These are the questions that used to live in the
`reflection` node's `questions` array, kept here so they can be pasted
into the slides or whatever platform hosts the actual reflection.

1. Which of the six endings did you land in? Does it match the kind of
   reasoning you'd actually use in real life, or did the situation just
   carry you there?
2. If you played again and picked differently early on, do you think
   you'd land in a completely different ending — or were some of these
   six outcomes within reach the whole time, regardless of the smaller
   choices along the way?
3. Small confession: however you answered "Do you tell anyone?" back at
   Step 1, you ended up here regardless. Now that you know, does that
   drain the choice of meaning — or does it say something real about how
   often a decision can feel high-stakes in the moment while the outcome
   never actually traces back to it?
4. Two people could take completely different paths through this story
   and both come out with an ending that's hard to call wrong. Is "there's
   no single right answer" a satisfying place to land, or does it feel
   like a dodge?
5. Pick the ending you feel least sure about. What's the strongest
   argument against the choice Scrap-E made there?

Students can revisit their own choices via the in-app "Review your paths"
button on the hand-off screen (tracks every attempt made this session —
choices plus which ending each one reached) before answering these
externally.
